# Table 1. Experimental design (publication scale, Phase 5)

Full factorial would be F in {3,5,8} x |A| in {5,10,20} x E in {1,2} x demand in {uniform, clustered, diurnal} (54 configs). The study uses an 18-config one-third fraction generated by the defining relation `demand_idx = (F_idx + |A|_idx) mod 3`, crossed with both E levels.

**Aliasing disclosure.** Under this fraction the main effects of F, |A|, and demand are aliased: no clean main effect of any of the three is identifiable from this grid. Marginal displays over F or |A| are therefore descriptive only; per-demand facets are the pre-registered presentation.
